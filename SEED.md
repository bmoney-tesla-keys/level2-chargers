# Seed list — next Level 2 home charger pages

Ship one sourced page at a time. Each page needs the manufacturer plus at least two independent reviews when they exist. Do not invent specs or prices.

These are the next seven models, in a suggested order. “Why” is about search demand and how often reviewers discuss them — not a ranking we invented.

1. **Autel MaxiCharger (AC Elite Home)** — CNET’s security-features pick (app or RFID lock, adjustable output, hardwire or plug-in). Frequently searched 50 A class unit.
2. **Tesla Wall Connector / Universal Wall Connector** — CNET’s overall pick and Car and Driver’s future-proof pick; the default comparison for Tesla households and mixed J1772/NACS garages.
3. **JuiceBox (Enel X Way / JuiceBox 48)** — CNET’s “green energy” pick, with scheduling and optional green-grid software. Long search history under the JuiceBox name.
4. **ClipperCreek** — Long-running residential/commercial EVSE line often specified by electricians as a durable, simpler hardwired option. Good “no-app” contrast page.
5. **Lectron (Level 2 Wi-Fi and V-Box Pro)** — Car and Driver’s 2026 budget pick (portable Wi-Fi Level 2) and CNET’s no-frills V-Box Pro pick. Covers the inexpensive end of the market.
6. **FLO Home** — Network-branded home charger from a major North American public-charging operator; natural parallel to ChargePoint’s “same app at home and in public” pitch.
7. **Siemens VersiCharge** — Legacy electrical-brand home EVSE that electricians and utility rebate lists still mention; useful for shoppers who want a familiar panel-brand name.

When a page is written, move it out of this list and add a `LOG.md` line with the path and sources used.
